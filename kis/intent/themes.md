# Themes

Seven, all dark, built 2026-09-20 (`slice-23-ui.md`). Bronze is the default and the one the app's
type and spacing were tuned against; the other six come from a list of light palettes the owner
supplied and are built the other way round -- **the palette's primary is the ground and its
accent stays the accent** -- which keeps no theme's accent sitting in its own background's hue
family. Marketing Citrus is the exception: its primary `#ea580c` is an orange, and an orange page
is not a page, so it takes stone-900 as the ground and keeps both colours as accents.

**Every value is generated by `frontend/tools/themes.py`.** Do not hand-edit a hex in
`styles.css`: a palette gives three colours, the app needs eighteen tokens on four surfaces, and
nothing in the stylesheet enforces a floor.

## The floors, and the surfaces they are measured on

- **4.5:1** for anything read: body, muted, the four tones, `--accent` and `--accent-2` as text,
  and each ink on the fill it sits on.
- **3:1** for what is not text: the star glyph and `--session` where it draws the session ring.
- Measured against **the surface the text actually sits on** -- the page, a card, and a card
  header -- and for a chip, against **its own colour at 12% over the card**, which is what a chip
  paints behind its text. That composite is worth about 0.4 of a ratio point and is the margin
  several of these palettes have.
- The colour that goes **on** a filled control is the ground (`--accent-ink`, `--session-ink`).
  A fill its ink cannot sit on is *brightened*, never darkened -- darkening it to satisfy the ink
  is what collapsed the session ring to 1.15:1 against its own card on the first attempt.

## Where the palettes came from

The six were given as light palettes with a primary, a neutral and an accent. Their Tailwind
names were resolved against Tailwind v3's published values: gray-900 `#111827`, gray-200
`#e5e7eb`, amber-500 `#f59e0b`, amber-600 `#d97706`, teal-600 `#0d9488`, slate-50 `#f8fafc`,
slate-800 `#1e293b`, orange-500 `#f97316`, emerald-600 `#059669`, yellow-50 `#fefce8`, stone-800
`#292524`. Fintech Classic, Marketing Citrus and Security Slate came with their own hexes.

## Notes for whoever changes this

- To change a theme: change its inputs in `tools/themes.py`, re-run it, paste
  `tools/out/themes.css` into the palette section of `styles.css`, and re-run the proof.
- **Prove a colour by what the browser paints, not by the token.** `--session-ink` was correct in
  every theme while `.session-bar .primary` still hardcoded `color: #fff` at 3.69:1, and a probe
  that compared tokens reported it as fixed. The harness now reads computed styles off real
  elements, composites translucent fills, and starts and stops a session in every theme to reach
  the one button that was wrong.
- An unchecked checkbox stays browser-grey in every theme: `accent-color` only paints the checked
  state, and the empty box comes from `color-scheme`.
