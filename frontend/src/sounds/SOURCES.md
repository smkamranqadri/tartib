# Session-end sounds

Kitchen and wood are CC0 (public domain); bell is under the Pixabay Content License. Credit is
not required for any of them; it is given anyway.

- `kitchen.mp3` -- "Mechanical alarm clock, ring #3", sound 2659 by Joseph Sardin,
  https://bigsoundbank.com/mechanical-alarm-clock-ring-3-s2659.html (CC0, see
  https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html). Trimmed to 4.6 s with a 0.6 s fade.
- `bell.mp3` -- "Bel Sekolah" (school bell), Pixabay sound 153453,
  https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/bel-sekolah-153453/, under the Pixabay Content License
  (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free to use in a project, no attribution
  required, not to be redistributed as a standalone sound. Chosen by the user. Trimmed to 3.6 s
  with a 0.3 s fade, made mono.
- `wood.mp3` -- `impactWood_light_000` to `_002` from Kenney's *Impact Sounds* 1.0,
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds (CC0): two runs of three knocks, 0.17 s apart.

All loudness-normalised with ffmpeg `loudnorm`, mono MP3 96k.
