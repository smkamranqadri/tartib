# Session-end sounds

Every sound here is CC0 (public domain). Credit is not required; it is given anyway.

- `kitchen.mp3` -- "Mechanical alarm clock, ring #3", sound 2659 by Joseph Sardin,
  https://bigsoundbank.com/mechanical-alarm-clock-ring-3-s2659.html (CC0, see
  https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html). Trimmed to 4.6 s with a 0.6 s fade.
- `bell.mp3` -- "Bell #2", sound 2114 by Joseph Sardin,
  https://bigsoundbank.com/bell-2-s2114.html (CC0). Low-passed at 3.5 kHz to warm it, trimmed to
  5 s with a 0.8 s fade.
- `wood.mp3` -- `impactWood_light_000` to `_002` from Kenney's *Impact Sounds* 1.0,
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds (CC0): two runs of three knocks, 0.17 s apart.

All loudness-normalised with ffmpeg `loudnorm`, mono MP3 96k.
