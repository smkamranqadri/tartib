# Session-end sounds

All three are built from CC0 (public domain) recordings in Kenney's *Impact Sounds* 1.0
(https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds, License.txt: "Creative Commons Zero, CC0"). Credit is
not required; it is given anyway. Arranged and loudness-normalised with ffmpeg, mono MP3 96k.

- `bell.mp3` -- `impactBell_heavy_000`, `_001`, `_000`, struck at 0, 1.1 and 2.2 s.
- `wood.mp3` -- `impactWood_light_000` to `_002`, two runs of three knocks, 0.17 s apart.
- `kitchen.mp3` -- `impactMetal_light_001` alternating with `impactTin_medium_000` every 70 ms,
  22 strikes a burst, two bursts 2 s apart: a kitchen-timer ring.
